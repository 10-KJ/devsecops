Ethics, privacy & limitations

  • Data privacy: student IDs are salted-hashed on ingestion; no names or contact details are needed; uploads are processed in memory and not stored.
  • Fairness: only academic-behaviour features are used. Gender, caste, religion, income and location are deliberately excluded.
  • Transparency: every flag lists plain-language reasons; the model is interpretable (logistic regression).
  • Human in the loop: output is a support signal, never a grade, label or disciplinary input.
  • Limitations: the prototype is trained on synthetic data. Real use needs validation on local data, bias audits, periodic re-training and consent.
